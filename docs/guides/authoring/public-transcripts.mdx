# Public transcripts

Scenario generation writes one public transcript per rendered scenario and
variant, at `dist/transcripts/public/guides/<guideId>/<scenarioId>[.<variant>].json`.
The contents are locked by the generator and schema tests rather than a
provider-backed run.

## What a public transcript excludes

Hidden setup never appears in public frames: `<Hidden>` blocks and hidden
steps are excluded, as are `render={false}` scenarios, which produce no
public transcript at all. Fixture wiring and copied paths — `<UseFixture>`
and fixture-copy paths — stay out of public output, and so do raw scenario
temp directories and internal event traces.

The internal transcript, at `dist/transcripts/guides/<guideId>/<scenarioId>.json`,
keeps full execution data for tests and is separate from the public artifact.
