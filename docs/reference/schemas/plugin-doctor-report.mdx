# Plugin Doctor Report

Public Lando schema contract for plugin-contributed doctor results.

[JSON Schema artifact](../../../dist/schemas/plugin-doctor-report.json)

| Field | Required | Type | Description | Default | Accepted values | Examples | Deprecation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `name` | Yes | `string` | Plugin-local check name, limited to 128 characters. | — | — | — | — |
| `status` | Yes | `string` | Check outcome used by doctor summaries and exit status. | — | `pass`, `warn`, `fail` | — | — |
| `severity` | Yes | `string` | Diagnostic severity associated with the check outcome. | — | `info`, `warn`, `error` | — | — |
| `runtimeStatus` | No | `string` | Optional human-readable runtime status, limited to 2,000 characters. | — | — | — | — |
| `runtime` | No | `object` | Optional structured runtime state. | — | — | — | — |
| `context` | Yes | `object` | Diagnostic context with at most 32 entries, 128-character keys, 2,000-character values, and 16,000 total value characters. | — | — | — | — |
| `solutions` | Yes | `array` | Zero to 16 remediation options for the reported condition. | — | — | — | — |
| `preempts` | No | `boolean` | Whether this report prevents provider construction and supersedes later checks. | — | — | — | — |
